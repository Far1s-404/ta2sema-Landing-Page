Delete your accountمسح حسابك

You can delete your ta2sema account yourself, inside the app.

  1. Open the app and go to Profile, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. The app shows what will be cancelled, refunded and lost. Confirm and type DELETE.

What is deleted

Your name, phone number, photo, profile, email, social accounts and notification tokens are erased and you are signed out. Your upcoming bookings are cancelled under the refund and cancellation rules.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Wallet credit left is given up and is not paid out.
  • An unpaid fine is first taken from your wallet. If some is still unpaid, we keep your phone number, the unpaid amount and the date for up to 90 days. This is removed when you pay it or after 90 days.
  • Booking and payment records are kept for accounting and fraud prevention without pointing at you as a person.

Cannot open the app?

Write to us from the contact page from the email address or phone number linked to your account, and ask us to delete it.
